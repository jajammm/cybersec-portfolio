I'm looking for a junior penetration tester role. I recently finished a Red Team bootcamp. Its final project was a full black-box web application assessment of an intentionally vulnerable e-commerce app in an authorized lab. That assessment produced 10 findings: 4 Critical, 4 High and 2 Moderate. The case studies on this site walk through it.

## What I do

- **Web application testing**: injection, broken access control (IDOR), session management, authentication flows and business logic.
- **Reconnaissance**: DNS and technology fingerprinting, port scanning, content discovery and manual crawling to map the attack surface before touching anything.
- **Reporting**: CVSS 3.1 scoring, reproducible proof of concept, business impact written for non-technical readers, and remediation developers can actually apply.

## Methodology

I follow the [OWASP Web Security Testing Guide](https://owasp.org/www-project-web-security-testing-guide/). I work inside agreed rules of engagement and keep evidence for every claim. I chain findings only when the chain changes the risk.
